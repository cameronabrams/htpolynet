Publications Using HTPolyNet
============================

These papers by other groups used htpolynet to build the systems they studied.
Most are not thermosets, and several are not even crosslinked networks in the
usual sense. The list comes from a reading of every work that cites
:cite:t:`Huang2023` as of November 2026; a paper that used htpolynet without
citing it would be missing here. If yours is missing, please open an issue.

- J. A. Godinez-Leon *et al.*, "Cyclodextrin polymers efficiently incorporate
  linezolid through multiple binding modes: insights from experiments and
  molecular simulations," *Carbohydrate Polymers* **393**, 125978 (2026).
  `doi:10.1016/j.carbpol.2026.125978 <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.carbpol.2026.125978>`__
  -- an epichlorohydrin-crosslinked cyclodextrin drug carrier, with its
  composition taken from NMR and SEC.

- D. Wei, F. Zeng, J. Cui, and Y. Wang, "Multiscale analysis of sulfur-bridge
  topology and linear mechanical response in natural rubber," *Journal of
  Molecular Modeling* **32** (2026).
  `doi:10.1007/s00894-026-06906-1 <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00894-026-06906-1>`__
  -- sulfur-vulcanized cis-1,4-polyisoprene.

- T. Lee, C. S. Ng, N. Li, J. Zheng, and P.-C. Su, "Molecular origins of the
  paradoxical crosslinker-length effect on rubber elasticity in crosslinked
  polymer networks for vat photopolymerization," *Additive Manufacturing*
  **122**, 105209 (2026).
  `doi:10.1016/j.addma.2026.105209 <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.addma.2026.105209>`__
  -- IBOA/PEGDA photopolymer networks.

- Z. Thebault, F. S. Sorce, S. Ngo, and A. Taylor, "Modelling thermomechanical
  properties of thermoset coatings using crosslink density extracted from
  molecular dynamics simulations via network mapping," SSRN preprint (2025).
  `doi:10.2139/ssrn.5709823 <https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.5709823>`__
  -- polyester-melamine coatings, including simulated tensile tests and
  *T*:sub:`g`.

- N. AL-Hamdani, P. V. Saravia, J. Luque Di Salvo, S. A. Paz, and G. De Luca,
  "Unravelling lithium interactions in non-flammable gel polymer electrolytes:
  a density functional theory and molecular dynamics study," *Batteries*
  **11**, 27 (2025).
  `doi:10.3390/batteries11010027 <https://doi.org/10.3390/batteries11010027>`__
  -- PEGDA-crosslinked gel polymer electrolytes.

- H. Sun *et al.*, "Eutectogel-based biomimetic barrier with
  network-structure-transformation-activated defensive and reparative
  functions for infected wounds," *Materials Horizons* **12**, 7986-7999 (2025).
  `doi:10.1039/d5mh00788g <https://doi.org/10.1039/d5mh00788g>`__
  -- a eutectogel network.

- L. Orszulak *et al.*, "Inhibition of naproxen crystallization by polymers:
  the role of topology and chain length of polyvinylpyrrolidone
  macromolecules," *European Journal of Pharmaceutics and Biopharmaceutics*
  **207**, 114581 (2025).
  `doi:10.1016/j.ejpb.2024.114581 <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejpb.2024.114581>`__
  -- linear and star polyvinylpyrrolidone for amorphous solid dispersions.

- F. Gholami *et al.*, "Fast and efficient fabrication of functional
  electronic devices through grayscale digital light processing 3D printing,"
  *Advanced Materials* **36** (2024).
  `doi:10.1002/adma.202408774 <https://doi.org/10.1002/adma.202408774>`__
  -- a GMA/AUD/PEGDA printing ink, built with a modified version of the
  algorithm.
